# `store-platform` — asset requirements

What the merchant must supply, and what the storefront guarantees in return.
Written because **the media treatment in `design-system.md` §12 is conditional on
photography the merchant controls**, and a platform sold to more than one
merchant cannot leave that implicit.

**Every unconfirmed line is `[ASSUMPTION]`.** The register is
`../../docs/store/03-decisions-and-assumptions.md`.

---

## 1. The finding that makes this document necessary

Rendered and measured, not assumed: on the **dark ground**, a photograph with
mean relative luminance around **0.084** dissolves into the ground exactly as
intended, while one around **0.484** — a product shot on a white sweep —
**punches out as a hard bright rectangle**. No ring or shadow fixes it, because
the rectangle itself is bright.

**This is a §17-class defect**: it passes for a merchant who shoots dark and
fails for a merchant who shoots on white. Both are completely normal for apparel
and for hardware.

**A merchant shooting on a white sweep is the normal case, not the exception.**
That is the assumption that failed. Studio product photography on white is the
default for apparel and for hardware — it is what a catalogue shoot produces, what
a supplier supplies, and what a marketplace export contains. A design that only
works for dark, in-situ photography is a design that works for the minority.

**Resolved — but not by the fix that was first accepted.** The original
recommendation (imagery on `--sf-surface` rather than `--sf-canvas`) was
implemented and **does nothing**: the image is `object-fit: cover`, so it fills
its container and the container background is never visible. A ring on the dark
ground failed too — a light ring is invisible against a bright photograph.

**What works is an inset edge-darkening applied to the image on the dark ground
only**, fading the photograph's outer edge into the ground. A white sweep stops
reading as a hard rectangle; the product's centre is untouched, so colour
fidelity is preserved; a dark photograph is unaffected and still dissolves.
Details and the trade-off in `design-system.md` §12.

Still invisible to the merchant, and it does not constrain what they may shoot —
which was the point.

The rejected alternative was to mandate a background treatment per merchant —
a business constraint on the customer rather than an engineering choice, and one
that produces a worse store precisely when it is ignored.

## 2. Product photography

| Requirement | Value |
|---|---|
| Aspect ratio | **4:5 portrait**, ratio-locked. The grid must never reflow on load |
| Minimum long edge | `[ASSUMPTION]` 1400px, to survive a 2× 700px card |
| Format | AVIF or WebP, with a JPEG fallback generated by the pipeline |
| Colour space | sRGB. A P3 original will shift unpredictably per browser |
| Background | **Consistent within a merchant** — recommended, not enforced. White sweep is expected and fully supported; the dark ground absorbs it (§1). Mixing white-sweep and in-situ shots in one grid looks inconsistent, but it no longer *breaks* anything |
| Per-variant imagery | Optional; falls back to the product gallery. A colour axis without per-variant images is a worse purchase experience but not a broken one |
| Count | `[ASSUMPTION]` 1 required, up to 8 per product, up to 4 per variant |
| Alt text | **Authored in the backoffice, per locale, never generated.** Decorative imagery gets empty `alt` |

**Hand tools and hardware carry one extra requirement:** at least one image must
establish **scale** — in the hand, or beside a rule. A chisel photographed alone
against a sweep is unbuyable, and "42 mm" does not fix it for most people. This
is a merchandising requirement, not a technical one, and it belongs in this
document because nothing in the code can enforce it.

## 3. Brand assets

| Asset | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Logo | SVG preferred; must be legible at 24px height and on **both grounds** — a single-colour logo that vanishes on near-black is a common failure |
| Favicon | `[ASSUMPTION]` 32px and 180px raster plus SVG |
| OG image | 1200×630, per locale, **text baked in at a size that survives a feed's own compression** |
| Hero media | 16:9 minimum, and it must tolerate a bottom scrim without losing its subject — the scrim is where type sits |
| Brand colours | Fills only. Validated against both grounds at write time, falling back to the derived value. See `design-system.md` §8 |

---

## 4. What the storefront guarantees in return

- **A ratio-locked container**, so nothing shifts as images load. Layout
  stability is a hard requirement, not a nicety.
- **A pre-composed scrim** for any type over media, clearing 4.5:1 against an
  image whose brightness nobody controls.
- **A designed fallback** — a missing image renders a token-coloured surface, not
  a broken-image glyph or a collapsed card.
- **A degraded-brand path** — if the store-profile read fails, the storefront
  renders its own measured neutral palette rather than unstyled text.
